Multi-Point Polar Align

Plate-solve 5–10 photos while rotating only the RA axis. More points make the fitted axis more resistant to a bad solve or slight phone movement.

Add sky photos

On a phone, the first button opens its camera. On a laptop, use the import button to select a photo copied from your phone.

Capture sequence

  1. Attach the phone rigidly to the telescope or finder.
  2. Focus the phone camera at infinity if possible.
  3. Point near the meridian, roughly 45–70° high.
  4. Take Photo 1.
  5. Rotate only RA by 15–30°.
  6. Take Photo 2.
  7. Rotate only RA another 15–30° in the same direction.
  8. Take Photo 3.
  9. Do not touch Alt/Az bolts until the result appears.
